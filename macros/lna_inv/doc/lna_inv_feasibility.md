## What the numbers say

**4.36 dB of noise figure at 3.6 mA, and more current buys little.**
The pair carries 54 % of the output noise, the feedback resistor 9 %, the shunt coil 1 %, and the sweep in [`lna_inv_sweep.md`](lna_inv_sweep.md) shows the floor: 5.8 dB at 2.5 mA, 4.4 at 4, 4.0 at 6 and 4.0 at 8, so current buys nothing past 6 mA.
The estimate, 1 + RS/Rf + gamma/(gm RS), reads 1.6 dB at 4 mA and is 2.8 dB low at 4 and 6 mA and more at lower current, which is the PSP RF model's gate and substrate network on 150 and 300 um of device and the noise gain of a shunt-feedback stage that the estimate does not carry. The bench is the number.

**What it is good at.** S11 -28 dB with nothing tuned, IIP3 +5 dBm, P1dB -9 dBm, no coil on chip and no gate coil off chip, only the shunt coil the pad needs anyway. The match depends on the load: the sweep solves the feedback for the bench's 200 ohm and for 100 ohm, and a real load reads differently.

**No selectivity.** With nothing tuned it rejects 8.2 dB at 5 GHz off F0 (`selectivity_db` in [`lna_inv_results.json`](lna_inv_results.json)). What shape there is comes from the pad network, nothing from the amplifier, so any filtering the application needs has to come from elsewhere.

## Method and limits

- Sizing: `scripts/lna_inv_sizing.py` on the gm/Id tables, the pair at its own trip point, VGS = VDS = 0.6 V, so the current sets both widths and the feedback resistor is solved for 50 ohm against the load. The match depends on the load, and the bench's 200 ohm is a stand-in for the next stage.
- Small-signal (`lna_inv_tb_sp`) runs the PSP RF model, `rfmode=1`. Harmonic balance (`lna_inv_tb_hb`) runs `rfmode=0`.
- The bench's 200 ohm load stands in for the next stage, and its thermal noise is excluded from the NF row.
- Corners: the 45-point CACE set in [`lna_inv_corners.md`](lna_inv_corners.md), ngspice, small signal only. The numbers above are TT, 27 C, and the supply current alone spans 0.32 to 12.53 mA across that set, so read the corner doc before quoting any row here as the block's behaviour.
- Part and parasitic spread: [`lna_inv_robust.md`](lna_inv_robust.md), the nominal design with one thing moved at a time.
- IIP3 and P1dB are TT only. They need harmonic balance, which the CACE flow does not run.
- Not covered: layout parasitics, the pad itself beyond its capacitance, and whatever follows the LNA.
